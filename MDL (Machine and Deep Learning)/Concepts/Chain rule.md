# Chain rule

The derivative of a composition is the product of the derivatives along the path; with several paths, sum over them.

$$\frac{dz}{dx}=\frac{dz}{dy}\frac{dy}{dx},\qquad\frac{d}{dt}f(x(t),y(t))=\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt}+\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt}$$
