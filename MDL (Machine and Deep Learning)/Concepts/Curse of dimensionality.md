# Curse of dimensionality

The number of examples needed can grow exponentially with the number of features. Most volume sits in the rim of a high-dimensional sphere, and density estimation becomes hopeless.

$$10^p\text{ histogram cells},\qquad\frac{\mathrm{Vol}(0.9r)}{\mathrm{Vol}(r)}=0.9^p\to0$$
