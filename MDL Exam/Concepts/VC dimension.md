# VC dimension

The largest number of points a classifier can shatter, that is, label in every possible way. A proper measure of complexity; $p+1$ for a linear classifier in $p$ dimensions.

$$h_{\text{linear}}=p+1,\qquad h\le\min\big(\lceil R^2/\rho^2\rceil,p\big)+1$$
