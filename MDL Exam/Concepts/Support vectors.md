# Support vectors

The training objects with non-zero Lagrange multiplier $\alpha_i$. They lie on the margin and fully determine the classifier.

$$w=\sum_i\alpha_iy_ix_i,\qquad\varepsilon_{LOO}\le\frac{\#SV}{N}$$
