# Bar notation

Shorthand $\bar v=dL/dv$ for the computed derivative of the loss with respect to a node.

$$\bar y=y-t,\quad\bar z=\bar y\sigma'(z),\quad\bar w=\bar zx,\quad\bar b=\bar z$$
