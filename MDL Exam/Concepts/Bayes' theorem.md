# Bayes' theorem

Rewrites the posterior in terms of the class-conditional density and the prior. The denominator follows from the law of total probability.

$$p(y\mid x)=\frac{p(x\mid y)\,p(y)}{p(x)},\qquad p(x)=\sum_i p(x\mid y_i)p(y_i)$$
