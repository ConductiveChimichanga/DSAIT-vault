# Gaussian distribution

A blob-shaped density with mean $\mu$ (location) and covariance $\Sigma$ (elliptical shape).

$$p(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^p\det\Sigma}}\exp\!\big(-\tfrac12(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)\big)$$
